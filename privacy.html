Privacy Policy

Last updated August 2026

dhaaga is a small, independent project — a way to send an AR Rakhi. Here's exactly what happens with your information when you use it.

The short version

We don't collect, store, sell, rent, or trade your personal information. There's no account, no database, and no server behind this site beyond static file hosting — there's nothing to collect it into.

The camera

When you open a Rakhi link and grant camera access, hand-tracking runs entirely on your own device, in your browser. Your camera feed is never uploaded, recorded, or transmitted anywhere — not to us, not to any server. It never leaves your phone.

Names and notes

When someone sends a Rakhi, the recipient's name, sender's name, and any note are stored only inside the link itself (as part of its web address) — never on a server or in any database we control. Anyone with that link can read it, so treat it the way you'd treat any message you're sharing, and only send it to the person it's meant for.

Third-party services

This site loads a few things from other providers, purely to run:

  • Google Fonts, for the typefaces on this page
  • MediaPipe (a Google-provided hand-tracking model), loaded from a public CDN and run entirely on your device
  • GitHub Pages, which hosts these files as static pages

None of these receive your name, note, or camera feed from this site.

Children's privacy

dhaaga is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has shared personal information through this site, please reach out and we'll address it.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated with a new date above.
