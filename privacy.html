Privacy Policy

Last updated August 2026

dhaaga has no backend. No account, no database, no server logic — just static pages on GitHub Pages and a camera that never sends anything anywhere. That's not a summary of a longer policy; it's the whole reason this page can be this short.

What actually happens when you send a Rakhi

The recipient's name, your name, and your note aren't saved to a database — because there isn't one. They're encoded directly into the link you get after picking a design, and they only ever exist inside that link. Whoever opens it can read them (the same way anyone can read a text message you forward), so only send it to the person it's for. Delete or stop sharing the link, and that's the end of it — there's no copy sitting on a server, because it was never sent to one.

What actually happens when you open one

Hand-tracking runs locally, in your phone's browser, using a WebAssembly model loaded once from Google's public CDN. Your camera feed is decoded frame-by-frame on-device to find your wrist — it is never uploaded, saved to disk, or transmitted off your phone, by dhaaga or by anyone else. Closing the tab or tapping the exit button ends it; nothing persists after that.

Who else is involved

Three outside services, each doing exactly one job and receiving nothing personal from you:

  • Google Fonts — serves the typefaces on this page
  • MediaPipe (Google) — the hand-tracking model, run entirely on your device
  • GitHub Pages — hosts these files as static pages

None of them see your name, your note, or your camera feed — that data never leaves the browser tab to reach them in the first place.

A note on the logo typeface

dhaaga is a personal, non-commercial project made as a creative tribute to Raksha Bandhan — it is not built or operated for profit. Some visual elements, including the typography in the logo, are used for personal, non-commercial expression while a proper license is sorted out. If you are a rights holder with concerns about how any element is used here, please reach out via LinkedIn and it will be addressed promptly.

Children

This isn't built for or marketed to children, and it doesn't knowingly collect anything from anyone under 13. If you're a parent and think a child has shared something through a dhaaga link, reach out below and it'll be sorted out.

If this changes

Adding a real backend, analytics, or any data collection would be a big enough change that this page — and the date at the top — would be updated first, not after.
